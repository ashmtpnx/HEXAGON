# NyaySetu Design System — Official Government Theme

> Version 1.0 | September 2026
> A clean, minimal, professional design system for a Government of India platform.

---

## Color Palette

### Primary (Navy Blue)
- `primary-700`: `#0f2744` — Hover states, dark accents
- `primary-600`: `#1a3a5c` — Navigation, headings, primary buttons
- `primary-500`: `#24537a` — Links, focus rings
- `primary-400`: `#3b6d96` — Secondary elements
- `primary-100`: `#dae8f4` — Light tinted backgrounds
- `primary-50`: `#eef4f9` — Very light tint

### Accent (Saffron)
- `accent-600`: `#e06518` — Accent button default
- `accent-500`: `#f37021` — CTA highlights, active indicators
- `accent-100`: `#fde8d4` — Tinted alert backgrounds

### Success (India Green)
- `success-500`: `#138808` — Approved, success states
- `success-100`: `#d4edda` — Success tint

### Danger (Red)
- `danger-500`: `#dc2626` — Rejected, errors
- `danger-100`: `#fee2e2` — Error tint

### Neutrals
- `surface-50`: `#f7f8fa` — Alternate section bg
- `surface-200`: `#e2e5e9` — Borders, dividers
- `surface-500`: `#64748b` — Secondary text
- `surface-800`: `#1e293b` — Headings
